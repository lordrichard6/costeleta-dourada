# Alternative Logo Prompts for Costeleta Dourada

Here are 5 detailed prompts you can use in AI image generators (like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or others) to create a new logo.

Accompanied by the text "COSTELETA DOURADA" in a sharp, luxury serif font (like Cinzel or Bodoni). Solid white background. Flat design, no shading, suitable for gold foil embossing. gold color code #C6A45C.

## 2. Rustic Alentejo (Traditional Vibe)
> **Prompt:** Vintage badge style logo for a traditional Portuguese restaurant. Centerpiece is a detailed illustration of a Cork Oak tree (sobreiro) with a pork cutlet integrated into the roots or trunk. Colors: Warm earthy browns, deep greens, and antique gold. Text "Costeleta Dourada" curved around the top in a rustic heritage typeface. Weathered texture effect, white background.

## 4. Artistic & Organic (Farm-to-Table Vibe)

The text "Costeleta Dourada" written in an organic, flowing brush script. White background, visible paper texture.

## 5. Emblem & Crest (Official Vibe)
> **Prompt:** Heraldic crest logo design. A shield shape divided into two sections: one showing a golden wheat field, the other a stylized pork cutlet. Topped with a crown of cork branches. Royal gold and dark brown color scheme. "Costeleta Dourada" written in a bold, authoritative serif font below the crest. Vector style, clean lines, white background.

## Tips for Generation
- **Aspect Ratio**: If using Midjourney, add `--ar 1:1` for a square or `--ar 3:2` for a wider layout.
- **Transparency**: AI generators usually create a solid background. You will need to use a tool like Photoshop or an online "Background Remover" to make the white background transparent.
- **Vector**: Adding "vector" or "flat design" helps prevent the AI from adding too much 3D shading, making it easier to use as a logo.
