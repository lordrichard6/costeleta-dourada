# Dish Image Prompts for Costeleta Dourada

Here are detailed prompts to generate high-quality, consistent food photography for your menu items. They are designed to match the "Rustic Luxury" aesthetic of the website.

**General Settings (for Midjourney/DALL-E):**
> *Parameters:* Photorealistic, 8k, warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field (bokeh), rustic wooden table background, ceramic or clay plates, Alentejo vibe.

---

## Prato do Dia (Daily Specials)

### 1. Segunda: Cozido de Grão
> **Prompt:** A hearty Portuguese stew "Cozido de Grão" presented in a traditional clay bowl. Rich broth with chickpeas, chunks of pork meat, traditional sausages (chouriço), potatoes, and pumpkin. Steam rising, garnished with fresh mint leaves. Rustic wooden table setting with a slice of cornbread on the side. Warm, inviting atmosphere, high resolution food photography.

### 2. Terça: Sopa de Cação
> **Prompt:** Authentic "Sopa de Cação" (Dogfish Soup) in a rustic deep ceramic plate. A rich, yellowish garlic and coriander broth with thick slices of white fish and slices of Alentejo bread soaking in the sauce. Fresh coriander garnish. Soft natural lighting, elegant but traditional presentation.

### 3. Quarta: Migas com Entrecosto
> **Prompt:** Detailed food shot of "Migas Alentejanas com Entrecosto". A mound of golden, savory bread-based migas, surrounded by crispy, golden-fried pork ribs (entrecosto). Served on a traditional hand-painted terracotta plate. Garnish of orange slices and parsley. Rich textures, appetizing and oily sheen on the meat.

### 4. Quinta: Ensopado de Borrego
> **Prompt:** "Ensopado de Borrego" (Lamb Stew) served in a deep earthenware dish. Tender pieces of lamb stewed in a rich, reddish-brown sauce, resting on top of toasted slices of rustic country bread. Fresh mint sprigs on top. Steam rising, warm golden hour lighting hitting the sauce.

### 5. Sexta: Bacalhau Dourado
> **Prompt:** "Bacalhau Dourado" presented on a pristine white ceramic plate. A golden, creamy mixture of shredded codfish, matchstick potatoes, and eggs, garnished generously with black olives and chopped parsley. Fluffy texture, soft lighting, elegant restaurant composition.

### 6. Sábado: Carne de Porco à Alentejana
> **Prompt:** Classic "Carne de Porco à Alentejana". Cubes of fried marinated pork mixed with clams in their shells and cubed fried potatoes. Rich sauce, pickles and coriander garnish. Served in a copper or clay cataplana bottom. Vibrant colors, savory and delicious look.

---

## Ementa (Menu Items)

### 7. Secretos de Porco Preto
> **Prompt:** "Secretos de Porco Preto" grilled to perfection. Strips of black pork meat with visible grill marks, juicy and glistening with fat. Served on a slate or rustic wood board with a pinch of flower salt and sprigs of oregano. Side of small roasted potatoes. High contrast, focus on the meat texture.

### 8. Açorda Alentejana
> **Prompt:** Traditional "Açorda Alentejana" soup. A clear broth with distinct garlic, coriander, and olive oil base, large pieces of soaked rustic bread, and a perfectly poached egg in the center. Fresh bright green coriander floating. Served in a traditional clay bowl. Top-down or 45-degree angle.

### 9. Sericaia com Ameixa
> **Prompt:** Dessert "Sericaia". A slice of soft, creamy egg pudding with a cracked, cinnamon-dusted top. Served on a small dessert plate with a "Elvas Plum" (Ameixa d'Elvas) in syrup on the side. Golden, sweet, and traditional texture. Soft window light.

### 10. Pão de Rala
> **Prompt:** "Pão de Rala" dessert. A small, round almond cake dusted with flour/sugar, sliced open to reveal the golden egg yolk and gila filling (fios de ovos). Placed on a vintage doily or rustic cloth. Intricate texture detail, sweet and inviting.
